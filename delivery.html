How Delivery Works

  1. Select your preferred products from the catalogue.
  2. Complete the order-request form with your details.
  3. Choose a delivery method and preferred date.
  4. Wait for the shop to confirm stock, charges, and timing.
  5. Follow the agreed collection or delivery arrangements.
  6. Check the items when you receive them.

Returns Policy

Customers requesting a return should observe these conditions:

  • Contact customer support before returning an item.
  • Provide proof of purchase or order confirmation.
  • Keep the product, accessories, and packaging where possible.
  • Report damaged or incorrect items promptly.
  • Allow the shop to inspect the product and review the request.
  • Return eligibility depends on the confirmed shop policy and applicable law.

Warranty Claims

Some products may include a manufacturer's warranty. Warranty duration and coverage depend on the specific item and the terms confirmed at purchase.

  1. Keep your receipt and warranty documentation.
  2. Contact customer support to describe the problem.
  3. Provide the product model and available serial number.
  4. Follow the authorised inspection or repair procedure.

Warranty coverage may exclude damage caused by misuse or unauthorised repairs, subject to the actual warranty terms.
