Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

Browse the product catalogue, select your product, and complete the order-request form. Wait for the shop to confirm your request.

How long does delivery take?

The illustrative policy lists 3–5 business days for standard delivery and 1–2 business days for express delivery. Actual arrangements must be confirmed.

Can I collect my order from the shop?

Shop pickup is listed as a sample option. Contact customer support to arrange collection.

How do I request a warranty claim?

Keep your proof of purchase and warranty documents, then contact customer support for guidance.

Are the listed product prices real market prices?

No. The prices in this examination project are fictional sample prices provided for practicing HTML.
